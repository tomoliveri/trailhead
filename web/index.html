Marathon training
on your Garmin.

Tell it your race and the time you’ve got each week. It builds the plan, puts a coach on your watch, and changes the plan as you train.

Set up your planFree · no account · about 7 minutes

Today’s session, first thing.

Open the app and it shows what to do today, when, and for how long. Strength and mobility sit underneath when they’re on.

Heart-rate zones as you run.

The ring shows your target zone for each step. Drift above it and the watch buzzes. The zones screen shows where the session has been spent.

Pace targets and progress.

Each rep has a pace range and a bar that shows whether you’re in it. The strip along the top shows where you are in the whole session.

Knows when to turn home.

On out-and-back runs it tracks how far you are from the start, how long the plan has left and how much you usually slow down later on, then tells you when to turn so you finish where you started.

A plan that keeps up.

Every run the watch records feeds back in. Miss a day, run long, feel flat: the next sessions change to suit, and the predicted finish moves with you.

Three steps

  1. Your race. Date, goal, and roughly how much you run now. Sign in to Garmin Connect if you want your recent runs used.
  2. Your week. How long you’ve got each day. The plan fits inside it.
  3. Your watch. Plug it in and the app installs. Type the code it shows and you’re paired.

Open source

The plan is worked out in your browser, not on someone else’s server. There are no accounts: you get a private link to your plan. Your Garmin password is passed to Garmin and never kept. All the code is on GitHub.
